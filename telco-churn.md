# Telco Customer Churn Prediction

**Goal:** Predict customers at high risk of churn using machine learning and provide actionable retention strategies.

### Project Overview
Developed classification models (Random Forest & XGBoost) on a Telco dataset to identify churn patterns while preserving customer privacy.

### Key Results
- Identified contract type as a major predictor of churn
- Built models focused on business impact and customer retention
- Provided clear visualizations for stakeholders
